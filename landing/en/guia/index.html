Getting started

Getting Purgito up and running takes less than two minutes. Everything is controlled visually from the web Dashboard, with no complicated commands.

1

Invite Purgito

Add the bot to your server, authorizing permissions to read messages, send messages, embeds, and emojis.

2

Open the Dashboard

Log in with your Discord account at purgito.app and open your server's admin panel.

3

Enable learning channels

For privacy, Purgito starts with an empty channel list. In the Chat > Channels tab, select the text channels it should learn from.

4

Configure responses

Choose which channels it can reply to mentions in or speak up spontaneously, and set the frequency you want.

5

Let Purgito learn

As members chat in the enabled channels, Purgito builds its memory and starts talking with your server's characteristic style.

How Purgito learns

Purgito's normal response generation runs locally through Markov chains that link real words and idioms from your server. For the specific meme-caption feature, there's an optional integration with Groq (with automatic fallback to Markov if it's not configured or doesn't respond).

Chat messages — New messages sent by users in authorized channels.
↓
Data cleanup — Bots are discarded and mentions, links, and IDs are automatically stripped.
↓
Server corpus — Local, isolated memory that stores the learned text fragments.
↓
Markov model — Links word sequences based on how often they're used in your community.
↓
Generated response — Coherent output that mimics your community's style, humor, and vocabulary.
Full isolation

Every server has its own, independent corpus. What Purgito learns on one server is never shared with or affects another.

Local chat generation

Chat doesn't send conversations to external providers: replies are processed locally and mathematically through Markov chains.

Activation threshold

Requires a minimum amount of messages in the corpus before it can speak. The more active the chat, the richer and more varied its responses.

Chat and Responses

Configure how and when Purgito joins in on your server's text channels.

Mentions and Replies

When a user mentions @Purgito or replies directly to one of its messages, the bot evaluates its memory and produces an immediate response based on the server's corpus.

Spontaneous replies

Purgito can join the chat on its own initiative, without a direct mention. You can adjust how often this happens through the cadence and probability settings in the panel.

Balanced probabilities

For every reply, the bot balances whether to generate a phrase with Markov, use a fixed special phrase, or add a GIF, according to the probabilities you configure.

Per-channel settings (Overrides)

Customize behavior per channel: let it talk a lot in casual channels (#spam, #general) and restrict it in serious channels, or mute it entirely where needed.

Corpus (Server memory)

The corpus is the database of messages learned on your server. It's the core of Purgito's vocabulary and personality.

Authorized channels

Purgito only stores messages from channels you explicitly add to the allowed list. Channels not selected are completely ignored.

Privacy filter

Before a message is stored, external links, user and role mentions, and IDs are stripped to keep memory clean and private.

Automatic rotation (FIFO)

Once the storage limit is reached (10,000 messages on the Free plan / 50,000 on Premium), the system drops the oldest messages proportionally across channels.

Reset and maintenance

You can clear or reset the corpus at any time from the Dashboard's Chat > Data tab if you want Purgito to start learning from scratch.

GIF Gallery

Build a visual collection of your own for your community that Purgito can use in its replies, or that admins can manage from the web.

Interactive web gallery

Browse, preview, and organize every GIF saved on your server in a smooth interface from the panel's GIFs tab.

Multiple ways to add GIFs

Upload files directly, paste GIF URLs, or search and pick animations with the built-in Tenor search.

GIF replies

Turn on the GIF probability in the Chat panel so Purgito sends animations from your gallery in its replies.

Memes and Meme Bank Premium feature

Generate memes on the fly by combining visual templates saved by your community with the humor of Purgito's corpus.

/momo and /meme commands

Any member can use these Discord commands to generate an instant meme from the meme bank's images and the text the bot has learned.

Collaborative meme bank

Save templates and funny images by reacting to messages in Discord or uploading them directly from the Dashboard's Memes tab.

Automatic memes

Set up a meme channel so Purgito automatically posts generated memes at regular intervals, with no manual work needed.

Smart captions

If the server has the vision engine enabled, Purgito interprets the image's content to produce contextually funny punchlines.

Automatic reactions

Have Purgito interact with users' messages by reacting with the server's custom emojis.

Adjustable frequency

Control how often Purgito reacts (low, medium, or high) from the Chat > Reactions tab.

Server emojis

Uses the custom emojis created in your community so reactions feel natural.

Muted channels

Channels marked as muted automatically disable reactions so nothing gets interrupted.

Special phrases and Packs

Customize Purgito's replies with fixed punchlines, in-jokes, or themed messages exclusive to specific channels.

Special phrases

Fixed phrases written by admins. When replying, Purgito has a chance of picking a special phrase instead of generating new text with Markov.

Themed packs

Let you group phrases and assign them to specific channels. Phrases without a pack stay in the general pool, while ones assigned to a pack are only used in its linked channels.

Phrase without a pack → General pool → Available in every response channel
Phrase in a Pack (e.g. #welcome) → Restricted → Only triggers in the Pack's channels

Triggers (Automatic triggers)

Configure exact, deterministic replies for when a user types a keyword or key phrase in chat.

Deterministic vs Probabilistic

While Markov chat is random and generates text, a Trigger is 100% exact: if the message matches the criteria, Purgito replies instantly with the assigned response.

Match types

Choose whether the trigger fires when the message contains the word, requires an exact match, or looks for standalone keywords.

Embed and Component Editor

1

Design your message

Set title, description, accent color, columned fields, author, images, thumbnails, and footer.

2

Add interactive buttons

Add link buttons or modern components (Components V2) to link to websites, social media, or server actions.

3

Preview live

See exactly how your message will look in Discord, in real time, before publishing it.

4

Send or Schedule

Publish instantly to the channel you pick, or schedule it for a specific date and time.

Server Events (Welcome, Goodbye & Boosts)

Configure fully customizable automatic messages when members join, leave, or boost your server.

👋 Welcome

Greet new members with direct mentions, ordinal count ("Member #1,284"), account age, and avatar.

🚪 Goodbye

Say goodbye to departing members with customizable plain text or rich embeds.

🚀 Boosts

Celebrate boosts instantly with variables for boost tier, total count, and boosts remaining until the next level.

💡 3 Content Modes & Dynamic Placeholders

Each event can be sent as a Normal message, Classic embed, or Layout V2 with interactive components. Use the variable engine {user}, {server_name}, {server_membercount_ordinal}, and more with real-time live preview.

YouTube alerts

Get automatic alerts on your Discord server every time your favorite YouTube channels publish a new video.

Link a channel

Enter the YouTube channel's URL or ID in the panel's YouTube tab.

Target channel

Pick the Discord text channel where Purgito will post the alert for each new video.

Custom mentions

Set a role to mention (e.g. @Notifications or @everyone) alongside the alert message.

Scheduled announcements

Automate reminders, community notices, and recurring messages without sending them manually.

Frequencies and intervals

Schedule messages to go out at fixed intervals (every X hours or days) or on a specific date and time for special events.

Format and channels

Send plain text or rich embed messages to the Discord channels you designate, to keep your server informed.
