Generation Engine

Purgito uses a hybrid generation architecture: a custom first- and second-order probabilistic Markov chain engine for chat conversations, and an optional AI vision integration (Groq) for automatic meme captioning.

Markov Engine Mechanics

The Markov engine (src/markov_engine.py) statistically models word sequence transitions observed in the server's message corpus:

  • Tokenization and States: Training text is normalized and split into word tokens while preserving punctuation boundaries. Two transition dictionaries are constructed: a second-order model (word pairs (w1, w2) -> w3) to preserve grammatical flow and complex slang, and a first-order model (w1 -> w2) acting as a fallback when a second-order state reaches a dead end.
  • Sentence Generation: Chains begin by picking a weighted initial token, advancing through states according to observed transition frequencies, and terminating upon reaching an ending token or the configured length limit (capped at Discord's 2,000 characters limit).
  • Post-processing Filters: Dangling conjunctions ("and", "or", "but", "of") at sentence endings are trimmed, malformed emoji syntax is stripped, and capitalization and whitespace are adjusted.

Corpus Management and Ingestion

Each server maintains an isolated corpus stored in the SQLite corpus table:

  • Ingestion Sanitization: Before database insertion, incoming messages pass through regex filters stripping user mentions (<@id>), role mentions (<@&id>), channel links (<#id>), HTTP/HTTPS URLs, and code blocks.
  • Channel Allowlist: Only channels explicitly marked on the reading allowlist feed the corpus. If a channel is placed on the silenced list, it is ignored entirely for both learning and automatic replies.
  • Per-User Corpus: Messages are also tagged with the author's user_id in the user_corpus table, enabling dedicated models for commands that mimic specific community members.

Concurrency Control and Caching

Markov model training and sequence generation are CPU-bound operations. To prevent performance degradation across concurrent guilds, Purgito enforces strict resource governance:

  • Global Asynchronous Semaphore: An asyncio.Semaphore limits concurrent model compilation and generation tasks process-wide.
  • Non-blocking Spontaneous Replies: Spontaneous responses (triggered by conversation activity cadences) attempt non-blocking semaphore acquisition: if slots are saturated, the response is dropped silently to keep the bot responsive. Direct mentions and replies wait in queue.
  • In-Memory LRU Cache: Compiled transition models are cached in an LRU (Least Recently Used) dictionary per guild and user. Models are invalidated automatically upon cleanup operations (amnesia) or substantial new data ingestion.

Trimming Policies and Quotas
