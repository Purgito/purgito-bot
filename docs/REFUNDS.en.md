# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** August 2, 2026

This page summarizes how the free trial, cancellation, and refunds work
for Purgito's Premium plan.

## Free trial

There's a 7-day free trial on the monthly plan. The trial applies once
per customer (same buyer or payment method), even if activated on a
different server.

## Cancellation

The subscription is canceled from the Polar customer portal, access to
which is emailed (by Polar, not Purgito) when you subscribe. It can't be
canceled from the Purgito dashboard.

## Refunds

Purgito doesn't offer refunds for periods already started or paid for,
whether monthly or annual.

Before paying, every server has access to a 7-day free trial on the
monthly plan, specifically meant for deciding whether Premium is worth
it before committing to a payment. Because of that, once a paid period
has started, no partial or full refunds are issued — not for the
remaining time, not for how much the service was used, and not for a
later decision to stop using it.

Canceling the subscription is different from requesting a refund:
canceling only stops the next period's automatic renewal. The Premium
access you already paid for stays active as normal until that period
ends — it isn't cut short, and you also don't get money back for the
remainder of that period if you cancel before it ends.

Since payments are processed by Polar.sh as Merchant of Record, any
payment dispute (chargeback) is handled directly between whoever paid
and Polar, under Polar's own terms — this can lead to Premium access
being suspended while the dispute is open (see "Revocation").

## Revocation

Purgito's developer or Polar.sh may revoke Premium access in cases of
fraud, chargeback, or unresolved payment failure.

## What happens to your content if the server loses Premium

When a server stops having Premium (due to cancellation, revocation, or
the end of the paid period), the content it had already saved (message
corpus, GIFs, embed templates, etc.) **isn't deleted**. It simply stops
being able to keep growing beyond the free plan's limits: if you already
had more content saved than the free plan allows, that surplus is kept
as-is until, through normal use of the bot, it naturally converges to
the free limit (for example, the system discards the oldest content as
new content is saved, in the categories that already work that way).

## Who Premium belongs to

Premium activated on a server belongs to that server (identified by its
Discord guild_id), not to the person or account that made the payment.

In practice:

- If whoever paid leaves the server, is kicked, or transfers ownership
  of the server to someone else, Premium stays active with no changes —
  the server doesn't lose its benefits.
- Managing the subscription (canceling, changing plans, viewing
  receipts) can only be done by whoever has access to the Polar customer
  portal tied to that purchase — normally, whoever originally paid. If
  that person loses access to their email or Polar account, no one else
  on the server can manage the subscription directly; you'll need to
  contact the developer to resolve it case by case.
